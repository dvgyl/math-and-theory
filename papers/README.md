# Research papers

Every retained result lives under a subject and a coherent result family. A
family contains its manuscript, source, citations, proof package and checking
instructions. Companion results are kept with their main family; the Motzkin
bounded-forcing paper is one example. The Gyires family also contains its
certificates, completed formal cases and explicitly unfinished extensions.

| Subject | Collection |
|---|---|
| Mathematics | [Matrix theory, graphs, sequences, inequalities and optimization](mathematics/) |
| Theoretical computer science | [Algorithms, Boolean functions, allocation and proof complexity](theoretical-computer-science/) |
| Mathematical physics | [Quantum information and stochastic thermodynamics](physics/) |

Use the [complete catalogue](../CONTENTS.md) for abstracts and exact result
scopes. The [shared Lean library](../lean/) serves the seven families listed in its proof map;
other families have their own pinned Lean package beside the manuscript.
[Common verification tools](../verification/) check the collection as a whole.
See [adding a result](../docs/ADDING_RESULTS.md) for the repository conventions.
