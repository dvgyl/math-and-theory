# Authorship and disclosure

This work is fully autonomous agentic research. Dev D. Goyal is responsible for publication. No expert validation or external peer review is claimed.

Autonomous AI research agents found the results and their proofs, wrote the exact and floating-point checkers, wrote the Lean development for Lean 4.19.0 and searched the literature in an earlier session, starting from the open question of Ducuara, Susa and Skrzypczyk (arXiv:2406.03945). The records of that session describe internal audits carried out within the same automated workflow; they do not name the system used and are not included.

Opus 5.5 by Anthropic used with Claude Code assessed the package for this release. It checked the source question and the cited equations against arXiv:2406.03945, read the proofs of the main theorem, the rational violating branch and the Galois obstruction, reran all seven checkers from a copy, and wrote and ran an independent brute-force comparison over complex filters. It rebuilt the Lean project module by module with a new whole-project axiom audit, checked the formal statement against actual density matrices and filters, compared the reused Lean library with the repository's steering-bounds package, edited the manuscript and integrated the package into this repository. The written proofs of the extensions were read but not changed. The original amalgamated-build tooling, internal audit notes and logs are not included. AI checks are not human peer review.

The question and the measures are credited to Ducuara, Susa and Skrzypczyk. Steering ellipsoids and canonical filtering are credited to Jevtic, Pusey, Jennings and Rudolph and to Milne et al. The spectral trust-region method is credited to Gander, Golub and von Matt and to Adachi, Iwata, Nakatsukasa and Takeda. The Lean library in `lean/QuantumPrior` is adapted, with attribution, from the author's earlier steering-ellipsoid formalization; it contains the fully-entangled-fraction bound that is Conjecture 2 of Milne, Jennings, Jevtic and Rudolph, for which the Omega Institute's public repository records an earlier claimed Lean proof (8 October 2026, issue #14206) and for which no priority is claimed. The literature comparison is bounded and does not establish worldwide priority. The package is distributed under the Apache-2.0 license.
