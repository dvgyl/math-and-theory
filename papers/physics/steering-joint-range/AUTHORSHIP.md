# Authorship and disclosure

This work is fully autonomous agentic research. Dev D. Goyal is responsible for publication. No expert validation or external peer review is claimed.

Autonomous AI research agents found the results and their proofs, wrote the Lean development for Lean 4.19.0 and Mathlib, wrote the exact and numerical checkers and a draft report, and searched the literature in an earlier session, starting from the earlier package [steering-bounds](../steering-bounds/). The records of that session describe internal reviews carried out within the same automated workflow; they do not name the system used.

Opus 5.5 by Anthropic used with Claude Code assessed the package for this release. It read the proofs of the joint interval, the canonical interval, the probability frontier and the volume bounds, compared the principal Lean statements with the mathematics, reran every checker from a copy, wrote the exact check [exact_new_results.py](calculations/independent/exact_new_results.py), added the whole-project axiom audit, ran the fresh build recorded in [verification/](verification/), read the Omega Institute's two public dossiers on the steering-centre conjectures, edited the manuscript from the draft report and integrated the package into this repository. The earlier session's internal reviews, replay logs, compatibility shims and the original inputs are not included. AI checks are not human peer review.

The question is from Ducuara, Susa and Skrzypczyk (arXiv:2406.03945); the steering-ellipsoid framework, canonical filtering and maximally obese states are credited to Jevtic, Pusey, Jennings and Rudolph and to Milne et al.; the fixed-centre volume maximum to Milne, Jevtic, Jennings, Wiseman and Rudolph; trust-region theory to Moré–Sorensen and Fortin–Wolkowicz. The Omega Institute's earlier public record of a claimed Lean proof of the fully entangled fraction bound is acknowledged, and no priority is claimed for that bound. The literature comparison is bounded and does not establish worldwide priority. The package is distributed under the Apache-2.0 license.
