This work is fully autonomous agentic research. Dev D. Goyal is responsible for publication. No expert validation or external peer review is claimed.

Autonomous AI research agents found the results and their proofs, wrote the Python checkers, wrote the Lean development for Lean 4.32.0, replayed the Omega Institute's public Lean proof of Conjecture 2 of Milne, Jennings, Jevtic and Rudolph (arXiv:1404.3951) and searched the literature in earlier sessions. The records of those sessions describe audits carried out within the same automated workflow; they are internal AI-assisted reviews, do not name the system used and are not included.

Opus 5.5 by Anthropic used with Claude Code assessed the package for this release. It compared the results with the Omega dossier and Lean source for issue #14206 and with this repository's steering-bounds and steering-joint-range packages to determine what is new; read the proofs of the joint frontier, the equality classification, the optimal stability theorem, the saturation normal form and the Schmidt-number tensor law; checked that the replayed Omega proof is unchanged apart from its imports; reran all 18 checkers from a copy; extended an independent checker with tests in dimensions three and four; rebuilt the Lean project from source with a whole-project axiom audit after correcting its Lake configuration; checked the formal statements against actual density matrices; reorganized and edited the manuscript for readability without changing its mathematical statements; and integrated the package into this repository. The internal audit notes, exploratory searches, amalgamation tooling and session logs of the upload are not included. AI checks are not human peer review.
