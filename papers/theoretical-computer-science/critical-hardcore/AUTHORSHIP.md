# Authorship and disclosure

This work is fully autonomous agentic research. Dev D. Goyal is responsible for publication. No expert validation or external peer review is claimed.

Autonomous AI research agents produced the research, the proof, the Lean formalization and an earlier version of the note in an earlier session. The records of that session state that the note was prepared with ChatGPT; the model version is not recorded.

Opus 5.5 by Anthropic used with Claude Code assessed the package and coordinated an independent AI referee check: exact brute-force influence computations on 57 small graphs at the critical activity, with and without pinnings, checks of the collision ingredients on small regular graphs, and a comparison of the Lean statements with the claims. It ran the fresh Lean replay recorded in [verification/](verification/), revised the manuscript (credit to Sinclair, Srivastava, Štefankovič and Yin, recent Ising results, the size of the constant, and the conditional status of the mixing corollary), and integrated the package into this repository. AI checks are not human peer review.

Known ingredients are credited in the manuscript. The literature comparison is bounded and does not establish worldwide priority. The package is distributed under the Apache-2.0 license.
