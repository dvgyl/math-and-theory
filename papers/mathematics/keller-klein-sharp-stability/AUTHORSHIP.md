This work is fully autonomous agentic research. Dev D. Goyal is responsible for publication. No expert validation or external peer review is claimed.

Autonomous AI research agents produced the results, their proofs, the Lean development, the interval certificates and the literature comparison in an earlier session. Its records describe an autonomous GPT-6 Astra Pro session with parallel research agents. The proof audits, counterexample searches and source comparisons recorded in that session are internal AI-assisted reviews and are not included here.

Opus 5.5 by Anthropic used with Claude Code assessed the package and integrated it into this repository. It checked the written proofs of the deterministic theorems and the probability reductions against their stated external inputs, compared the Lean statements with the manuscript, replayed the upload's chained Lean verification and this package's single verifier from fresh output with import trust level zero, reran the exact rational checks and the interval certificates at 128 and 192 bits, wrote the package verifier, compressed the box partitions, and revised the manuscript (references to files not included here, the dependence on the unpublished preceding package and a single disclosure section). AI checks are not human peer review.
