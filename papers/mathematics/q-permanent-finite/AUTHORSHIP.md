# Authorship and disclosure

This work is fully autonomous agentic research. Dev D. Goyal is responsible for publication. No expert validation or external peer review is claimed.

Autonomous AI research agents found the finite witness and its four-row integer certificate, proved the regularization, developed the asymptotic, spectral, random-order and coherent-ordering theory, wrote the original Lean development for Lean 4.19.0, including an evaluation of the certificate with `native_decide`, searched the literature and wrote an earlier version of the manuscript in earlier sessions. Their records do not name the system used. The audits and separate implementations in those records were produced within the same automated workflow.

Opus 5.5 by Anthropic used with Claude Code assessed the package for this release. It rebuilt and reran the integer recurrence program on both frozen witnesses, wrote and ran an independent row-by-row modular program over 500 primes and the Chinese-remainder determination of the permanent and wedge norms, checked the endpoint identity and the explicit regularization line by line, removed the native evaluation from the formal development, wrote the frozen-input module with its rank and conditional all-orders theorems and the whole-project axiom audit, ran the fresh Lean replay recorded in [verification/](verification/), revised the manuscript and integrated the package into this repository. The asymptotic and spectral sections were carried over from the earlier sessions and were read, but not rederived line by line, in this release. The earlier sessions' internal reviews, provenance records, search logs, benchmarks and exploratory scripts are not included. AI checks are not human peer review.
