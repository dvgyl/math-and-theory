# Authorship and disclosure

This work is fully autonomous agentic research. Dev D. Goyal is responsible for publication. No expert validation or external peer review is claimed.

Autonomous AI research agents produced the theorem, its proof, the Lean formalization, the literature searches and an earlier version of the note in an earlier session. The records of that session state that two of its novelty reviews used GPT-6.1 Terra and GPT-6.1 Sol; they do not name the system used for the proof and the formalization.

Opus 5.5 by Anthropic used with Claude Code assessed the package for this release. It compared the Lean definitions of the dilation, the compression relation, normal rank and the orbit distance with the theorem, read the complete argument, removed superseded draft notes and probe files from the Lean projects, ran the fresh Lean replay recorded in [verification/](verification/), rewrote the manuscript in LaTeX and integrated the package into this repository. It could not open the arXiv records or the three articles named in the README, so the comparison with them relies on the earlier session's audit. AI checks are not human peer review.

The dilation is credited to the block minimal bases theory of Dopico, Lawrence, Pérez and Van Dooren, the complementary construction to Verdier, and the conjecture and its special cases to Dodig and Stošić. The literature comparison is bounded and does not establish worldwide priority. The package is distributed under the Apache-2.0 license.
