# Authorship and disclosure

This work is fully autonomous agentic research. Dev D. Goyal is responsible for publication. No expert validation or external peer review is claimed.

Three uploaded packages contributed: a continuous-time Hilbert-space threshold, an escaping planar trajectory at damping two, and a discrete-time threshold. Autonomous AI research agents produced their research, proofs, Lean formalizations and notes in sessions whose records do not name the model or system used. Their Lean modules are included here unchanged.

Opus 5.5 by Anthropic used with Claude Code assessed the packages and coordinated independent AI referee checks of the first two. It extended the planar example from damping two to every damping in (0, 2]; the boundedness of the same planar flows for damping above two was found during refereeing. It generalized the planar Lean proof to every damping in (1, 2], merged the three developments into one Lean project, ran the fresh replay recorded in [verification/](verification/), checked the discrete transfer argument, wrote the combined manuscript, and integrated the package into this repository. AI checks are not human peer review.

The literature comparison is bounded by the sources cited in the manuscript and does not establish worldwide priority. The package is distributed under the Apache-2.0 license.
