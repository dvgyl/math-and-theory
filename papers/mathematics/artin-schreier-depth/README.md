# Depth-one Artin–Schreier defect towers are composita

**Fully autonomous agentic research.** Autonomous AI research agents produced the proof, the Lean formalization of the converse, the later Lean 4.35 formalization with Mac Lane–Vaquié depth and an earlier version of the note; Opus 5.5 by Anthropic used with Claude Code checked the argument against the source, formalized the forward implication and the equivalence in the source's own setting, ran a fresh Lean replay and rewrote the manuscript. Dev D. Goyal is responsible for publication. The [manuscript's disclosure section](manuscript.pdf) gives the details. No expert validation or external peer review is claimed.

Let `k` be algebraically closed of characteristic `p > 0` and let `K` be a perfect henselian subfield of the Hahn field `k((y^ℚ))` that contains `k(y)` and whose elements have no finite accumulation points in their supports.

**Theorem.** If a nontrivial finite Artin–Schreier tower `M/K` has depth one, then `M` is a compositum of Artin–Schreier extensions of `K`.

Nart and Novacoski proved the reverse implication and conjectured this one (*Depth of Artin–Schreier defect towers*, JPAA 230 (2026), 108184, arXiv:2503.18827v2, Conjecture 4.1), so a nontrivial finite Artin–Schreier tower over such `K` has depth one exactly when it is a compositum of Artin–Schreier extensions. The proof shows more: for every `θ ∈ M \ K`, the truncation of `θ` at the first finite accumulation point of its support generates a nontrivial elementary abelian subextension of `K(θ)/K`. The extraction step adapts an Ore-relation argument of Kedlaya (2017, Lemma 9.3).

## Formal scope

Both implications are formalized in two Lean projects.

- [lean_mlv/](lean_mlv/) (Lean 4.35) states the theorem as the source does. For every finite tower of Artin–Schreier steps over `K`, with the valuation ring of `K` henselian in Mathlib's sense, every embedding of the top field into an algebraic closure of `K` and every valuation there extending the Hahn valuation, depth one, defined as the least length of a complete Mac Lane–Vaquié chain over all primitive generators, holds exactly when the tower is generated by Artin–Schreier elements over `K` (`NTCThird.artinSchreierTower_intrinsicMLVDepth_one_iff` in [ASDepthTheorem.lean](lean_mlv/ASDepthTheorem.lean)). The chains are compared with the Okutsu criterion only at length one, which is all the theorem needs; no comparison at other lengths is claimed. The project vendors 54 modules of the NYLL library of Naganori Yamaguchi for henselian valuations.
- [lean/](lean/) (Lean 4.19) proves the equivalence in the algebraic closure of `K` inside the Hahn field, with depth one stated as the least length of an Okutsu sequence and henselianity as unique extension of the valuation (`okutsu_minimum_one_iff_asCompositum`). In this project the identification with Mac Lane–Vaquié depth is the source's Theorem 1.3.

## Prior work and priority

The root formula, support estimates, Krasner's lemma and the Ore relation are standard, and the minimal-face extraction is Kedlaya's. Searches in October 2026 found no other proof of the conjecture. The definitions and theorems cited from the source were checked against its arXiv source (version 2); the journal version could not be opened. Historical priority is not certified.
