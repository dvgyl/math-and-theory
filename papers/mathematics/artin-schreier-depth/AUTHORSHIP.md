# Authorship and disclosure

This work is fully autonomous agentic research. Dev D. Goyal is responsible for publication. No expert validation or external peer review is claimed.

Autonomous AI research agents produced the proof, the Lean formalization of the converse, the literature searches and an earlier version of the note in an earlier session. The records of that session do not name the AI system used.

A later session of autonomous AI research agents, continuing the same project, produced the Lean 4.35 project in [lean_mlv/](lean_mlv/): it formalized Mac Lane–Vaquié chains and their comparison with Okutsu sequences at length one, replaced the unique-extension hypothesis by henselianity of the valuation ring and proved the result for abstract towers. Its records do not name the AI system used either. That project adapts Lean sources of the Tau Ceti project and of Yijun Yuan's TrustworthyKedlaya, reuses one theorem of the Lean 4.19 project, and vendors 54 modules of Naganori Yamaguchi's NYLL library, whose author credits assistance by OpenAI Codex; [lean_mlv/THIRD_PARTY.md](lean_mlv/THIRD_PARTY.md) gives the pinned commits and notices.

Opus 5.5 by Anthropic used with Claude Code assessed the package for this release. It checked the argument step by step, read the arXiv source of the source paper and checked the cited definitions and theorems, compared the Lean endpoints and their hypotheses with the theorem, formalized the converse in the source's ambient field, the forward implication and their equivalence ([HahnClosureDepth.lean](lean/HahnClosureDepth.lean) and [HahnClosureForward.lean](lean/HahnClosureForward.lean)), ran the fresh Lean replay recorded in [verification/](verification/), rewrote the manuscript and integrated the package into this repository. For the Lean 4.35 project it compared the endpoints and the chain definitions with the theorem and with Nart's definitions, ran the fresh replay recorded in [verification/mlv/](verification/mlv/) and revised the manuscript; it did not write that project's code. AI checks are not human peer review.

The conjecture, the depth criterion and the forward implication are due to Nart and Novacoski; the minimal-face extraction is credited to Kedlaya.
