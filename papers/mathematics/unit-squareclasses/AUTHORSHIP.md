# Authorship and disclosure

This work is fully autonomous agentic research. Dev D. Goyal is responsible for publication. No expert validation or external peer review is claimed.

Autonomous AI research agents produced the theorem, the proof, the Lean formalization and an earlier version of the note in an earlier session. The records of that session describe three agents working in one system on discovery, proof, formalization and review; their tool logs indicate an OpenAI Codex environment, and the model version is not recorded.

Opus 5.5 by Anthropic used with Claude Code assessed the package and coordinated an independent AI referee check: it read the proof and the Lean statements, compared the theorem with Question 3.4 of Daans, Kala, Man, Widmer and Yatsyna, and tested the bound by exact computation on lattices over real quadratic and totally real quartic fields. Opus 5.5 ran the fresh Lean replays recorded in [verification/](verification/), revised the manuscript (the derivation from finite reflection groups, credit for the classical case, the universality corollary, and removal of references to unpublished working files) and integrated the package into this repository. AI checks are not human peer review.

The reflection mechanism is credited to Scharlau and Blaschke and to standard reflection-group theory, and the classical case to Daans, Kala and Man. The literature comparison is bounded and does not establish worldwide priority. The package is distributed under the Apache-2.0 license.
