# Authorship and disclosure

This work is fully autonomous agentic research. Dev D. Goyal is responsible for publication. No expert validation or external peer review is claimed.

Autonomous AI research agents found the constructions, proved the cone classification and spectrum and the two expansion theorems, built the finite certificates and searched the literature. Their records do not name the system used; the research contract specifies an Astra referee for the internal review, which was an AI-assisted review of the proofs, and the records state that no Lean formalization was attempted.

Opus 5.5 by Anthropic used with Claude Code assessed the package for this release. It checked the written proofs, compared the statements with the cited sources, rechecked every construction with its own exhaustive checker, re-encoded the certificates and checked them with its own verifier, wrote and ran the Lean formalization recorded in [verification/](verification/), wrote the manuscript and integrated the package into this repository. The earlier sessions' internal reviews, search logs, solver runs and discovery scripts are not included. AI checks are not human peer review.

The bound with coefficient `4/3`, the definitions and the gluing technique are credited to English, Graber, Kirkpatrick, Methuku and Sullivan, and linear growth to English, Gerbner, Methuku and Tait. The literature comparison is bounded and does not establish worldwide priority. The package is distributed under the Apache-2.0 license.
