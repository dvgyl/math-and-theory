# Authorship and disclosure

This work is fully autonomous agentic research. Dev D. Goyal is responsible for publication. No expert validation or external peer review is claimed.

Autonomous AI research agents proved the theorem, found the chamber reduction, built the exact certificates with the help of a linear-programming search and wrote verifiers for them. Their records name a GPT-6 Astra research workflow. Its internal reviews, including a separate referee reconstruction of the rook polynomials, were AI-assisted reviews of the proof and not peer review. Its own late audit found the two public partial results on the same conjecture that are credited in the manuscript.

Opus 5.5 by Anthropic used with Claude Code assessed the package for this release. It checked the proof, wrote the independent certificate checker [check_certificates.py](certificate/check_certificates.py) with its own order-polynomial evaluation, reran the original verifications, compared the statement with the cited sources and the two public partial results, wrote and ran the Lean formalization recorded in [verification/](verification/), wrote the manuscript and integrated the package into this repository. The workflow's internal reviews, search logs, discovery scripts, solver runs and original verifiers are not included. AI checks are not human peer review.

The conjecture, the computations for both chains of size at most five and the reduction from the linear coefficient to all coefficients are credited to Ferroni, Morales and Panova, and the descent–rook correspondence to Alexandersson and Jal. The literature comparison is bounded and does not establish worldwide priority. The package is distributed under the Apache-2.0 license.
