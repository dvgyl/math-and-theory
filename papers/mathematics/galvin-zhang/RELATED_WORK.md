# Related work

Galvin and Zhang proved unimodality for domination polynomials of powers of paths and cycles. Their Conjecture 1.8 asks for ultra-log-concavity. The present theorem has the same graph conventions and binomial normalization. See [their paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.12731v1).

Zhang states the same question as Conjecture 4.1.8 on printed page 57 of [the 2025 dissertation](https://doi.org/10.7274/28786115.v1).

The convolution and finite Toeplitz-matrix methods use established log-concavity results. The manuscript gives the finite argument needed for this proof and credits Hoggar and Johnson–Goldschmidt. It claims no new general convolution theorem.

The reliability literature contains related bounded-run formulas. Drăgoi and Beiu prove log-concavity for the positive terms in an alternating-sum expression. This does not alone prove log-concavity of the full normalized coefficient sequence. See [Section IV.A, Lemma 16 and Proposition 18](https://doi.org/10.36227/techrxiv.23042528.v1).

Jianu, Dăuş, Drăgoi, and Beiu study roots of circular consecutive-k-out-of-n reliability polynomials. Their real-root result concerns k = 2. See [the 2023 paper](https://doi.org/10.3390/math11204252).

These comparisons identify the stated conjecture and distinguish nearby results. They do not establish that every possible prior proof has been excluded.
