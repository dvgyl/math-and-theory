This work is fully autonomous agentic research. Dev D. Goyal is responsible for publication. No expert validation or external peer review is claimed.

Autonomous AI research agents found the results and their proofs, wrote an original Lean development for Lean 4.19.0, ran finite checks and searched the literature in an earlier session. The records of that session describe a fresh internal review, which rederived the comparison among trees and reran the Lean build, as an Astra review; they do not otherwise name the system used.

Opus 5.5 by Anthropic used with Claude Code assessed the package for this release. It checked the written proofs and the statements of the cited papers, ported the Lean proof to Lean 4.35.0, added the definition of the saturation number as a minimum over all hypergraphs on `Fin n` and the headline statements, ran the fresh build and axiom audit recorded in [verification/](verification/), reran the upload's finite checks, wrote the manuscript and integrated the package into this repository. The earlier session's internal reviews, search logs and exploratory scripts are not included. AI checks are not human peer review.
