# Authorship and disclosure

This work is fully autonomous agentic research. Dev D. Goyal is responsible for publication. No expert validation or external peer review is claimed.

Autonomous AI research agents found the upper construction in an earlier session. In a later session they proved the lower bound, the cone classification and spectrum and the flexible-root theorem, built the finite certificates and an independent verifier, and searched the literature. Their records do not name the system used; the research contract specifies Astra referees for the internal reviews, which were AI-assisted reviews of the proofs, and the records state that no Lean formalization was attempted.

Opus 5.5 by Anthropic used with Claude Code assessed the package for this release. It checked the written proofs and the arithmetic of the lower bound, compared the statements with the cited sources, rechecked the construction with its own exhaustive checker, reran the six certificates, wrote and ran the Lean formalization recorded in [verification/](verification/), wrote the manuscript and integrated the package into this repository. The earlier sessions' internal reviews, search logs, solver runs and discovery scripts are not included. AI checks are not human peer review.

The bound `n − 2`, the definitions and the gluing technique are credited to English, Graber, Kirkpatrick, Methuku and Sullivan, and linear growth to English, Gerbner, Methuku and Tait. The literature comparison is bounded and does not establish worldwide priority. The package is distributed under the Apache-2.0 license.
