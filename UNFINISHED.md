# Unfinished formal proofs

These items are not claimed as completed Lean results in this release.

| Result | Completed work | Remaining check |
|---|---|---|
| Gyires, general order-seven circulants | Written argument and exact computational certificates | Complete Lean proof of the actual matrix theorem, including all coefficient regions. |
| Gyires, rank at most two through order fifteen | Written argument and exact finite certificates | Complete Lean proof connecting the actual matrix representation, finite bounds, and contractions. |
| Higuchi–Sudbery four-qubit entropy bound | Mathematical argument, candidate formal sources, and exact certificate checks | Fresh complete source build and final direct statement checks. No checked release is included here. |
| Quartic entropy bound for rankings | Mathematical argument and candidate formal sources | Complete fresh source build and final direct statement checks. No checked release is included here. |

The Gyires manuscript includes the written arguments and identifies their formal verification limits. Complete Lean proofs are available for its principal order-five and order-six claims, all-order permutation polynomials, all-order four-block matrices, and the exceptional-vector theorem.

The unrestricted Gyires conjecture and the all-order rank-two problem remain outside these results. The thermal package proves finite spectral statements. It does not formalize the stochastic long-time identification theorem.
