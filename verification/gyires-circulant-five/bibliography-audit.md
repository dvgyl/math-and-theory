# Bibliographic details

Gyires's article “The common source of several inequalities concerning doubly stochastic matrices” appeared in volume 27 (1980), pages 291–304. The bibliography uses the year given by the journal. Crossref records a different year.

Falikman's entry cites the English translation and gives the original Russian page range. Egorychev's entry cites the 1981 *Advances in Mathematics* article.

The [Bolyai Society volume contents](https://www.bolyai.hu/files/kotetek_18_1978.pdf) give pages 471–484 for Gyires's 1978 article. This confirms the page range; it does not verify the article's proofs.
