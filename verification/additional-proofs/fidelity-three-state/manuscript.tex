\begin{abstract}
For an ensemble of three finite-dimensional quantum states, its pairwise
root fidelities form a positive matrix after weighting by the ensemble
probabilities. The entropy of this matrix bounds the ensemble's Holevo
quantity from above. The proof applies to arbitrary dimension, mixed and
singular states, and zero probabilities. It first proves a three-block
trace-norm comparison for entropy deficits. A simultaneous diagonal
attainment construction, the scalar three-by-three absolute-value entropy
comparison, and joint convexity of relative entropy give this comparison.
A weighted-root Gram matrix then gives the ensemble bound. The result is
the unrestricted inequality stated by Fannes, de Melo, Roga, and
\.{Z}yczkowski. A complete Lean formalization accompanies the proof.
\end{abstract}

\section{The ensemble bound}

The Holevo quantity bounds the classical information that a measurement
can extract from a quantum-state ensemble. The result below bounds it using
three probabilities and three pairwise root fidelities. The matrix in the
bound has size three regardless of the dimension of the states.

For a finite-dimensional positive semidefinite matrix $T$, use
\[
 \Ent(T)=-\sum_k \lambda_k(T)\log\lambda_k(T),\qquad 0\log0=0.
\]
This definition also applies to matrices whose trace is not one. All
logarithms are natural. Changing to any logarithm base greater than one
multiplies every entropy in the result by the same positive constant.

\begin{theorem}[Three-state root-fidelity entropy inequality]\label{thm:main}
Let $\rho_1,\rho_2,\rho_3$ be density matrices on any finite-dimensional
complex Hilbert space. Let $p_i\ge0$ and $\sum_{i=1}^3p_i=1$. Define
\[
 \sigma=\sum_{i=1}^3p_i\rho_i,
 \qquad
 C_{ij}=\sqrt{p_i p_j}\,
       \normone{\sqrt{\rho_i}\sqrt{\rho_j}}.
\]
Then $C$ is positive semidefinite with trace one, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:main}
 \Ent(\sigma)-\sum_{i=1}^3p_i\Ent(\rho_i)\le\Ent(C).
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

The matrix uses the \emph{unsquared} root fidelity. Under the convention
$F(\rho,\tau)=\normone{\sqrt\rho\sqrt\tau}^2$, its entries are
$C_{ij}=\sqrt{p_i p_j F(\rho_i,\rho_j)}$.
Equation~\eqref{eq:main} is the unrestricted inequality stated as
Conjecture~1, equation~(18), in the published Fannes--de Melo--Roga--\.{Z}yczkowski
paper~\cite{fmrz}. There is no assumption of commutativity, full rank,
strictly positive probabilities, qubit dimension, or compatible polar
unitaries.

The bound is attained when the state supports are mutually orthogonal.
Then the off-diagonal root fidelities vanish, $C=\diag(p_1,p_2,p_3)$,
and both sides of~\eqref{eq:main} equal $H(p)=-\sum_i p_i\log p_i$.
It is also attained when all three states coincide: the Holevo quantity
is zero and $C=vv^*$ for $v_i=\sqrt{p_i}$ has entropy zero.
These examples do not give a complete equality characterization.

\section{Scalar comparison in dimension three}

An elementary proof of a known ingredient follows. Its entropy
content occurs in the pure-state discussion of~\cite{fmrz}, using the
spectral monotonicity studied by Mitchison and Jozsa~\cite{mj}.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:scalar}
If $A\succeq0$ is a complex $3\times3$ matrix and $B=(|A_{ij}|)$, then
$B\succeq0$ and $\Ent(B)\ge\Ent(A)$. If $A\succ0$, then $B\succ0$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Write
\[
 A=\begin{pmatrix}a&u&v\\ \bar u&b&w\\ \bar v&\bar w&c\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The principal minors of orders one and two are unchanged upon replacing
entries by their absolute values. For the remaining principal minor,
\[
 \det B-\det A
 =2\bigl(|u w\bar v|-\operatorname{Re}(u w\bar v)\bigr)\ge0.
\]
The principal-minor criterion proves the claimed positivity statements.

First assume $A\succ0$. Let $x_1,x_2,x_3$ and $y_1,y_2,y_3$ be the
eigenvalues of $A$ and $B$. Their first elementary symmetric functions
agree because the traces agree. Their second elementary symmetric
functions agree because the sums of the principal minors of order two
agree. Their third elementary symmetric functions satisfy
$y_1y_2y_3\ge x_1x_2x_3$. Hence, for all $t\ge0$,
\[
 \prod_{k=1}^3(t+y_k)\ge\prod_{k=1}^3(t+x_k).
\]
Define
\[
 Q(t)=\sum_{k=1}^3\bigl((t+y_k)\log(t+y_k)
                       -(t+x_k)\log(t+x_k)\bigr).
\]
The derivative is the logarithm of the ratio of the two products, so
$Q'(t)\ge0$. For each fixed $x>0$,
\[
 (t+x)\log(t+x)=t\log t+x\log t+x+O(t^{-1})
 \quad(t\longrightarrow\infty).
\]
The common number of eigenvalues and their equal sums imply
$Q(t)\longrightarrow0$. Therefore $Q(0)\le0$, which is precisely
$\Ent(B)\ge\Ent(A)$.

For singular $A$, apply the positive definite case to $A+\varepsilon I_3$.
Its entrywise absolute-value matrix is $B+\varepsilon I_3$, since the
diagonal entries of $A$ are nonnegative. Continuity of $-x\log x$ at zero
and continuity of finite-dimensional eigenvalues give the limit as
$\varepsilon\downarrow0$.
\end{proof}

Write $\delta T$ for the ordinary diagonal matrix with the same diagonal
as $T$. In particular, $\delta A=\delta B$ in Lemma~\ref{lem:scalar}.
For positive definite matrices define the unnormalized relative entropy
\[
 \Rel(X\Vert Y)=\tr X(\log X-\log Y).
\]
The trace is real in this setting. Because $\log\delta A$ is diagonal,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:diag-deficit}
 \Rel(A\Vert\delta A)=\Ent(\delta A)-\Ent(A).
\end{equation}
Consequently Lemma~\ref{lem:scalar} gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:scalar-D}
 \Rel(B\Vert\delta B)\le\Rel(A\Vert\delta A)
 \qquad(A\succ0).
\end{equation}

\section{Simultaneous diagonal attainment}

The following construction is the one in Li and Zhang's proof of their
positivity theorem~\cite{lizhang}. Neither that construction nor the
positivity theorem is claimed as new here; Drury's earlier result is
credited in their paper.

\begin{lemma}[Li--Zhang construction]\label{lem:decomp}
Let $G=(G_{ij})_{i,j=1}^3\succeq0$ have square blocks of common size $d$.
There are unitaries $U_1,U_2,U_3$ such that, with
$U=U_1\oplus U_2\oplus U_3$ and $G'=UGU^*$, the scalar matrices
\[
 A_r=\bigl((G'_{ij})_{rr}\bigr)_{i,j=1}^3,
 \qquad B_r=(|(A_r)_{ij}|),\qquad 1\le r\le d,
\]
satisfy
\begin{equation}\label{eq:decomp}
 C=\sum_{r=1}^d B_r,
 \qquad C_{ij}=\normone{G_{ij}}.
\end{equation}
Every $A_r$ and $B_r$ is positive semidefinite. If $G\succ0$, they are
positive definite.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Successive block-unitary conjugations make blocks $12$ and $23$ positive
semidefinite: use their right polar decompositions, first changing block
coordinate $2$ and then block coordinate $3$. The second change leaves
block $12$ fixed. Write the resulting block $13$ as $PW$ with $P\succeq0$
and $W$ unitary. Polar factors can be extended to unitaries even when the
blocks are singular. Diagonalize $W$ by a common internal unitary basis
change in all three block coordinates. Blocks $12$ and $23$ remain
positive, while block $13$ becomes $\widetilde P\diag(z_r)$ with
$|z_r|=1$ and $\widetilde P\succeq0$.

Thus, for each block, the sum of the absolute values of its diagonal
entries equals its trace norm. For block $13$ this is
$\sum_r|\widetilde P_{rr}z_r|=\tr\widetilde P$; the other blocks and
their adjoints have the same property. Trace norms are unchanged by the
unitary transformations, giving~\eqref{eq:decomp}. Each $A_r$ is a
principal submatrix of $G'$. Apply Lemma~\ref{lem:scalar} to finish.
\end{proof}

In particular, $C\succeq0$ and $\tr C=\tr G$. The assertion that $C$ is
positive is established prior mathematics. The next section supplies the
entropy comparison used to reach the original target.

\section{The block entropy inequality}

Let $\Delta G=G_{11}\oplus G_{22}\oplus G_{33}$ be pinching by the three
outer block coordinates.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:block}
For every $G\succeq0$ with three square blocks of common size $d\ge1$, set
$C_{ij}=\normone{G_{ij}}$. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:block}
 \Ent(\delta C)-\Ent(C)
 \le \Ent(\Delta G)-\Ent(G).
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\emph{Positive definite case.} Assume $G\succ0$. Obtain $U,G',A_r,B_r$
from Lemma~\ref{lem:decomp}. Let $\Phi$ pinch the internal coordinate
$r$, retaining an entry between $(i,r)$ and $(j,s)$ exactly when $r=s$.
This is a finite unitary average. Explicitly, with
$\omega=\exp(2\pi\mathrm{i}/d)$, let
$Z_k e_{i,r}=\omega^{kr}e_{i,r}$ for $0\le k<d$. Then
\[
 \Phi(T)=\frac1d\sum_{k=0}^{d-1}Z_kTZ_k^*.
\]
The identity follows by summing the geometric progression
$\sum_k\omega^{k(r-s)}$.

The proof uses joint convexity and unitary invariance of quantum relative entropy.
These are established results, and their proofs are included in the
checked prior-foundation dependency bundle. They imply
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pinch}
 \Rel(G'\Vert\Delta G')
 \ge \Rel(\Phi(G')\Vert\Phi(\Delta G')).
\end{equation}
After reordering coordinates, the two arguments on the right are
$\bigoplus_r A_r$ and $\bigoplus_r\delta A_r$. Relative entropy is
additive over direct sums, since matrix logarithms act separately on the
summands. Therefore the right-hand side is
$\sum_r\Rel(A_r\Vert\delta A_r)$.

Equation~\eqref{eq:scalar-D} yields
\[
 \sum_r\Rel(A_r\Vert\delta A_r)
 \ge\sum_r\Rel(B_r\Vert\delta B_r).
\]
Joint convexity also yields the homogeneous finite-sum inequality
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sum}
 \Rel\left(\sum_r X_r\,\middle\Vert\,\sum_rY_r\right)
 \le\sum_r\Rel(X_r\Vert Y_r).
\end{equation}
Indeed, apply joint convexity to the uniform averages and multiply by
$d$, using $\Rel(tX\Vert tY)=t\Rel(X\Vert Y)$ for $t>0$.
Applying~\eqref{eq:sum} to $X_r=B_r$, $Y_r=\delta B_r$, and using
\eqref{eq:decomp}, gives the chain
\begin{align}\label{eq:chain}
 \Rel(G\Vert\Delta G)
 &=\Rel(G'\Vert\Delta G')\notag\\
 &\ge\sum_r\Rel(A_r\Vert\delta A_r)\notag\\
 &\ge\sum_r\Rel(B_r\Vert\delta B_r)\notag\\
 &\ge\Rel(C\Vert\delta C).
\end{align}
The first equality uses the fact that block-diagonal $U$ satisfies
$\Delta(UGU^*)=U(\Delta G)U^*$.

Finally, $\log\Delta G$ is block diagonal, so
\[
 \tr(G\log\Delta G)=\tr(\Delta G\log\Delta G).
\]
It follows that
$\Rel(G\Vert\Delta G)=\Ent(\Delta G)-\Ent(G)$.
Use~\eqref{eq:diag-deficit} for $C$ to turn~\eqref{eq:chain}
into~\eqref{eq:block}.

\emph{Singular case.} For $\varepsilon>0$ apply the proved result to
$G_\varepsilon=G+\varepsilon I_{3d}$. The off-diagonal blocks are
unchanged. For each diagonal block, positivity gives
\[
 \normone{G_{ii}+\varepsilon I_d}
 =\tr G_{ii}+d\varepsilon.
\]
Its norm-compression matrix is consequently
$C_\varepsilon=C+d\varepsilon I_3$. Also
$\Delta G_\varepsilon=\Delta G+\varepsilon I_{3d}$ and
$\delta C_\varepsilon=\delta C+d\varepsilon I_3$.
All four entropy terms converge as $\varepsilon\downarrow0$ by the
continuity of finite-dimensional entropy. This proves the assertion with
no rank or nonzero-block restriction.
\end{proof}

\section{Reduction to a three-state ensemble}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}]
Form the rectangular matrix
\[
 X=[\sqrt{p_1}\sqrt{\rho_1}\quad
    \sqrt{p_2}\sqrt{\rho_2}\quad
    \sqrt{p_3}\sqrt{\rho_3}],\qquad G=X^*X.
\]
Then $XX^*=\sigma$. The nonzero eigenvalues of $X^*X$ and $XX^*$
coincide, with multiplicity. Since zero eigenvalues contribute zero to
entropy, $\Ent(G)=\Ent(\sigma)$. Furthermore,
\[
 G_{ij}=\sqrt{p_ip_j}\sqrt{\rho_i}\sqrt{\rho_j},
 \qquad G_{ii}=p_i\rho_i.
\]
Thus its norm-compression matrix is exactly $C$ from the theorem.
Lemma~\ref{lem:decomp} proves $C\succeq0$, and $C_{ii}=p_i$ proves
$\tr C=1$.

Let $H(p)=-\sum_i p_i\log p_i$. Direct-sum additivity and the scaling
identity
$\Ent(p_i\rho_i)=p_i\Ent(\rho_i)-p_i\log p_i$ give
\[
 \Ent(\Delta G)=H(p)+\sum_i p_i\Ent(\rho_i),
 \qquad \Ent(\delta C)=H(p).
\]
The scaling identity is interpreted continuously at $p_i=0$.
Substitute these identities in Theorem~\ref{thm:block} and cancel $H(p)$:
\[
 H(p)-\Ent(C)\le H(p)+\sum_i p_i\Ent(\rho_i)-\Ent(\sigma).
\]
Rearrangement gives~\eqref{eq:main}.
\end{proof}

The block formulation is a useful structural form of the same result. It
is not counted here as a second independent research breakthrough. The
argument makes essential use of the three-by-three absolute-value lemma;
it does not establish an analogous statement for an arbitrary number of
states.

\section{Formalization and reproduction}\label{sec:lean}

\code{Fidelity.holevo_le_entropy_rootFidelityMatrix}.
Its hypotheses are exactly nonnegative real weights summing to one and
three positive semidefinite complex matrices of trace one on a common
finite index type. The formal proof derives nonemptiness of this type
from trace one. It imposes no commutativity, rank, dimension, or
polar-compatibility restriction.

Entropy is the spectral sum of $-x\log x$. The definition
\code{Fidelity.nuclear A} is the real part of $\tr\sqrt{A^*A}$;
its equality with the QIC trace norm on finite ordinal indices is proved.
The formal scalar comparison uses an exponential witness and the Gibbs
variational inequality for positive definite three-by-three matrices.
It supplies the positive definite part of Lemma~\ref{lem:scalar}.
The formal block proof handles singular matrices by regularization.
It does not formalize the derivative calculation printed here.

\section{Relation to prior work}

The inequality is Conjecture~1, equation~(18), of
Fannes, de Melo, Roga, and \.{Z}yczkowski~\cite{fmrz}.
Their paper gives the pure-state result and weaker bounds obtained by
reducing the off-diagonal fidelities. Zhang, Wu, and Fei~\cite{zwf}
prove the target under a compatibility condition on three polar
unitaries. The present statement has no such condition.

The scalar entropy comparison is known from the pure-state analysis
in~\cite{fmrz} and the spectral monotonicity in~\cite{mj}.
The simultaneous diagonal construction and positivity theorem are due to
Li and Zhang~\cite{lizhang}, who credit Drury's earlier positivity
result. Joint convexity, entropy continuity, and the Gram-spectrum
identity are also prior results. The entropy comparison for the block
trace-norm matrix is the step that connects these ingredients to the full
ensemble inequality. It is a structural form of the same result, rather
than a second independent contribution.

Related polar reduction, dephasing, and convexity methods appear in
Tang and coauthors~\cite{tang} for a different triangular-block entropy
inequality. Theorem~4.2 and Appendix~A of that source do not state the
three-block trace-norm comparison proved here.
Priority is not certified by the available literature review. In
particular, the complete texts of~\cite{km,sll,gy} were not available
for comparison. An accessible-information bound alone does not imply
a bound on the Holevo quantity. These access limits leave possible
overlap unresolved; they do not change the theorem proved here.
