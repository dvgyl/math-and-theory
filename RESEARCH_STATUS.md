# Result scope and unfinished work

The catalogue contains the retained completed mathematical statements, their manuscripts and reproducibility files. Some source comparisons remain provisional. A standalone theorem is not labelled a disproof of an external conjecture when the external definitions or proof class could not be confirmed. The papers state the exact internal definitions and assumptions.

The five selected Zenodo preprints are Higuchi–Sudbery, universal embezzlement, asymmetric Tutte, power-rank and multiplier-only ADMM. Their selection compares the original repository with the new results. It reflects scope and significance, not a priority certificate. The graph-root family and other retained papers remain available in the catalogue.

The new bounded-forcing Motzkin theorem extends the existing sequence family. The earlier root-log-concavity statement is supporting material, not a separate novelty claim. Elementary, known or duplicate observations are not added as independent research papers.

General order-seven circulants and rank-two orders nine through fifteen still have incomplete Lean coverage. Their written arguments and computational certificates remain distinguished from checked matrix theorems in UNFINISHED.md. The general circulant proof has an additional checked partial coefficient development, but that prefix does not prove the full matrix result. Unfinished zombie-game semantics and external source-identity questions are not presented as completed results.

Private inputs, prompts, internal review histories and runtime caches are not publication artifacts. This repository provides the curated scientific work and its proof boundaries.
