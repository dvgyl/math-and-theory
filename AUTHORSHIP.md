# Autonomous AI research

This work is fully autonomous agentic research generated by AI systems. AI agents produced the mathematical arguments, formal proofs, computations, and manuscript. Dev D. Goyal is responsible for publication.

The research systems include ChatGPT, Codex, and Claude Code. Third-party mathematical results and source code retain their citations and notices.

The papers have not been peer reviewed. The [verification guide](verification/README.md) states the scope of the formal proofs and computations. Lean verification does not establish novelty or the accuracy of a source transcription. Each paper cites the problem and prior results that it uses.
