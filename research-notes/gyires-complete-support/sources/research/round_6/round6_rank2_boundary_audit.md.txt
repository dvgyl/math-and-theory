# Independent audit of the rank-two Bernstein certificate

## Conclusion and scope

I independently checked the analytic reduction, homogeneous polynomial formulas, interval coverage, and equality argument in `round6_rank2_bernstein.md`. I found no gap. A separate exact rational checker also verifies the identities against direct permanent evaluations on 209 instances in orders 2–7, including interval endpoints.

This audit does not convert a finite range of verified orders into an all-order theorem. It is an independent AI mathematical audit and a finite exact implementation check, not human peer review or proof-assistant verification.

## 1. The stronger contraction has the required convex reduction

The contraction deficit is

\[
E(r,s)=\langle r,T_{\kappa(s)}r\rangle_B-\langle r,s\rangle_B.
\]

At fixed $s$, $T_{\kappa(s)}$ is positive semidefinite, so this is a convex quadratic form minus a linear functional of $r$. At fixed $r$, the matching mass and mean remove the degree-one noise component, leaving $1+\sum_{k\ge2}a_k^2\kappa(s)^k$. The coefficient $\kappa(s)$ is affine in the law at a fixed mean and belongs to $[0,1]$. Thus $E$ is convex in the second argument too. No joint convexity assertion is needed.

The Bernoulli-pair product parameter makes the law affine at fixed pair sum. A convex objective at a global interior maximum can be moved to either endpoint without losing maximality; choosing the endpoint that increases the sum of squared Bernoulli parameters proves the claimed shifted-binomial extremizer structure. Successive replacement of the two laws preserves a global maximum. The reduction therefore applies to contraction itself, not merely to the squared strong inequality.

## 2. Integer denominators and homogeneous degrees

Write $w=U+V$, $t=(LU+RV)/w$, and $\mathscr D=w^2t(n-t)$. The linear forms defining $X_k$ in equation (8) are exactly $nmw(x_i-t/n)$, so

\[
X_k=(nmw)^ku_k,\qquad Y_k=(n\ell w)^kv_k.
\]

Likewise $\mathscr K/(\ell\mathscr D)=\kappa_y$. Substitution into the permanent expansions gives

\[
C_x:=\operatorname{per}(A)/c_n
=\frac{\mathscr C}{n!m^n\ell^n\mathscr D^n},
\qquad
G_x:=\operatorname{per}(AA^T)/c_n
=\frac{\mathscr G}{n!m^{2n}\ell^n\mathscr D^{2n}}.
\]

Consequently

\[
C_x-G_x=
\frac{m^n\mathscr C\mathscr D^n-\mathscr G}
{n!m^{2n}\ell^n\mathscr D^{2n}},
\]

and

\[
C_x^2-G_x=
\frac{\mathscr C^2-n!\ell^n\mathscr G}
{(n!)^2m^{2n}\ell^{2n}\mathscr D^{2n}}.
\]

These are exactly equations (12) and (13), with strictly positive denominators whenever $0<t<n$. The degrees are $2n$ for $\mathscr C$ and $4n$ for $\mathscr G$ and both deficit numerators. Nonnegative ordinary homogeneous coefficients therefore imply nonnegativity on the entire interval, since $U,V\ge0$. Their signs agree with degree-$4n$ Bernstein coefficients because the conversion factors are positive binomial coefficients.

## 3. Coverage and degeneracies

Every genuinely nondegenerate shifted-binomial law has its mean strictly between its support endpoints. Thus a pair with a shared noninteger mean is covered by an enumerated interval with $L<R$.

A deterministic law at an integer $h\in\{1,\ldots,n-1\}$ can use profile $(h,1)$ with $t=h$. If the other law is nondegenerate, its upper support endpoint is at least $h+1$, so the resulting common interval has positive length. If both laws are deterministic, choose this same profile for both. Thus discarding zero-length intersections loses no required matrix case.

The omitted full-binomial profile makes one centered parameter vector zero, hence $A=J_n$ and contraction is equality. Endpoints $t=0,n$ are treated separately; approaching either endpoint forces every entry of (1) to $1/n$. All ordered profile pairs are retained, so the two orientations needed for $A$ and $A^T$ are covered.

## 4. Strictness for original Gyires

For nonuniform rank-two $A$, neither centered vector vanishes. Therefore $u_2=-\frac12\sum_i(x_i-t/n)^2$ is nonzero and $\kappa_y>0$. The $k=2$ term in the nonnegative Gram expansion is strictly positive, proving $\operatorname{per}(AA^T)>c_n$. Contraction then implies $\operatorname{per}(A)>c_n$, so the stated original-Gyires margin is strictly positive. Rank-one doubly stochastic matrices are uniform. This establishes the claimed equality classification within every order actually certified.

## 5. Independent exact implementation checks

`round6_rank2_boundary_audit.py` evaluates the homogeneous coefficient arrays and independently evaluates the original matrix permanents by subset dynamic programming over `fractions.Fraction`. It checks both cleared identities above at a deterministic selection of profile pairs in orders 2–7, with one interior parameter and both nondegenerate interval endpoints. All 209 identities passed. The report is `round6_rank2_boundary_audit.json`.

This implementation check verifies formulas and normalizations on the stated examples. The full finite theorem additionally relies on the complete integer coefficient enumeration and its recorded successful range, as described in the main note.
